LEADERSHIP & VISION

Peculiar Abokondu

Founder & Creative Director

"P$ NOIR was built on the intersection of raw street energy and uncompromising high-fashion craftsmanship. We don't make apparel for the masses; we craft statements for the select."

Hailing from Abuja, Nigeria, Peculiar Abokondu is the visionary behind P$ NOIR. With a background in both streetwear and high fashion, he brings a unique perspective to the brand's creative direction.
